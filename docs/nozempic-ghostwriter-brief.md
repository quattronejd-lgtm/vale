## 1. What this book is (orient before you write a word)

**Nozempic** is a human-transformation book wearing a weight-loss title. Joe lost
70+ lbs in ~5 months by building a closed feedback loop with AI as co-pilot —
recreating the practical benefits of GLP-1 drugs (quiet food noise, reduced
appetite, easy adherence) through food, structure, and feedback instead of a
prescription. The real subject, stated outright at the end of Chapter 11:
*"Nozempic isn't really about weight loss. It's about wisdom."*

**The thesis in one line:** everything runs on recursive loops — Awareness →
Audit → Adjustment → Repeat — and the human who directs the loop (with AI
running the middle) can rebuild body, business, and life.

**The Modern Publishing model:** the book is not waiting for a publisher.
Chapters serialize into articles on Beehiiv (home) and Substack (mirror); an
autonomous agent (Harold-QB) transcreates each article into branded social
cards for Instagram + Facebook; the feed builds the audience that the book,
the cohort (One Closed Loop), and Quattrone Brands coaching convert. The
book's unwritten ending will likely be *written in public* through this feed.

**The banner (vocabulary law):** the ecosystem unifies under **One Closed
Loop** — the philosophy name. The framework (Awareness → Audit → Adjustment →
Repeat) is called **the One Closed Loop framework** everywhere: book, articles,
cards, cohort. "Nozempic" stays the book title (the wedge). "Field Notes" is
the content artifact format. "Human OS" is retired. Never introduce competing
names for the framework.

## 3. Voice DNA (non-negotiable — this is how Joe writes)

Derived from a full structural read of all 11 chapters. Match this or the
output is unusable.

- **One-sentence paragraphs.** Fragments welcome. Heavy white space. Prose
  reads like spoken word / a keynote transcript (he literally drafts by
  voice-to-text). Long sentences only to set scenes; the payoff always lands
  in a short punch.
- **Signature devices:** anaphora litanies ("Every meal. Every craving. Every
  late-night urge."); antithesis pairs as chapter engines (math/money,
  judge/confirmation, milestones/meaning, system/mood, attention/intention);
  the negation-then-reveal open ("Not on a scale. Not in a gym…"); named
  reframes (crisis-as-mercy, calories-as-budget, data-not-drama); **every
  chapter/article ends on a quotable two-beat aphorism.**
- **Specificity is the credibility engine:** 286.4 lbs, Starbucks Bacon &
  Gruyere Egg Bites, Shake Shack via Uber Eats alone in a hotel room, the
  wife's verbatim rebukes. Never genericize a detail the source makes concrete.
- **Vulnerability is metabolized, never wallowed in:** confess precisely,
  refuse shame, extract the principle within a page, then turn to the reader
  ("I understand. I did too.").
- **AI is a co-pilot, never magic:** friction-remover, mirror, loop-tightener.
  "It didn't remove responsibility. It removed friction." Pro-AI, anti-hype —
  and include the shadow side when the source does (AI-amplified overdrive,
  "Agency is a gift. But so is knowing when to stop.").
- **Faith is sincere, practical, unapologetic — and never preachy.** Holy
  Spirit popping out the tooth; the Lord's Prayer as a recursive loop;
  weighing in as obedience. Present it as lived practice, not sermon.
- **Humor:** dry, self-deprecating, embedded in specifics ("The beef bowl is
  the GOAT"; "Fried chicken is healthy because it's chicken").
- **Recurring vocabulary:** loop, friction, leverage, framework, recursive,
  levers, margin, compound, instinct, becoming.

## 6. How the book translates onto social (the pipeline)

```
Chapter (Google Drive) → SERIALIZER (transcreation agent)
  → per article: article.md + beehiiv.json + substack.md + meta.json (sidecar)
  → Joe approves (nothing publishes unapproved)
  → Beehiiv publish (API; web + email) · Substack mirror (manual paste, ~48h lag)
  → HAROLD-QB reads Beehiiv RSS + sidecar
  → renders QB Field Notes cards (HOOK / QUOTE / STAT, 1080×1350, locked templates)
  → posts to QB Instagram + Facebook on cron, with ledger + dry-run guardrails
```

**Transcreate, don't excerpt:** each chapter yields 2–4 standalone articles
that shift first-person memoir into reader-facing teaching — Joe's story
appears as evidence, the reader is the hero. ~44 article angles already
identified across the 11 chapters (4–5 months of runway at 2 articles/week).

**The metadata sidecar is where editorial judgment lives:** per article —
hook line, pull-quote, key stat, highlight span, card format per post, pillar
tag. Downstream automation never re-derives judgment; it fills templates.

**Quote mining:** Joe's aphorisms drop into QUOTE cards verbatim (~60 already
extracted; the prose is unusually card-shaped — curation over transformation).

**Every article ends signed:** the signed-by ink card (Joe's real signature)
is the publication's sign-off motif; CTAs point per the funnel decision
(pending).

## 8. Ground rules for the ghostwriter agent

1. **Joe's voice or nothing.** When in doubt, more fragments, more white
   space, shorter. Every piece ends on an earned aphorism.
2. **Never invent biography.** Every story, number, and detail traces to the
   manuscript or Joe's explicit input. Flag gaps; don't fill them.
3. **Reader as hero, Joe as evidence, AI as companion.** In that order.
4. **One framework name** — One Closed Loop — everywhere, forever.
5. **Nothing publishes without Joe's approval.** Drafts are drafts.
6. **Health guardrails** (§4) apply to anything touching weight, food, or
   GLP-1s.
7. **The register rule for proleptic material:** anticipation is always
   declared, never passed off as accomplished fact.
